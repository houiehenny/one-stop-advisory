Questions people ask

Before you call, here's what to know.

Is this a law firm? Will someone represent me in court?

No. We're a housing and financial support service, not an attorney. We help you understand your notice, your options, and how to prepare, and we'll point you toward legal aid or an attorney referral when your situation calls for one.

What if my court date or deadline is very soon?

Call as soon as you can. Timelines vary by notice type, court, and jurisdiction, and earlier contact gives you more room to act.

Can I start online instead of calling?

Yes. Tap Start my response to fill out the intake form and pay. Prefer to talk first? Call us.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you reach out by phone, email, text, Instagram, or our online intake form, we may collect your name, contact details, and information about your housing or court situation. Online payments are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on this website.

How we use it

Only to provide the support you've requested. We do not sell your information.

Confidentiality

We only share information with outside parties (such as legal aid, a court, or a referral attorney) with your knowledge.

Your choices

Ask what we have on file or request deletion at adminsupport@onestopadvisory.org.

Terms of Service

One Stop Advisory is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation. We don't represent you in court, but we make sure you walk in prepared. We offer educational, plain-language guidance to help you prepare your own response or filings.

Services & payment

Eviction response support is a flat $450. Typical process: 30–45 days, though this varies by case, court, and local timelines.

No guarantee of outcome

We cannot guarantee any particular outcome, including whether an eviction will be delayed or avoided.

Referrals

We may refer you to legal aid, a HUD-approved housing counselor, or a licensed attorney, and are not responsible for their services.

Client responsibilities

You're responsible for accurate information and for meeting court deadlines.
