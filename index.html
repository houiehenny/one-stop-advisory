Questions people ask

Before you call, here's what to know.

What's the difference between eviction response and eviction removal?

Eviction response is for an active notice — it's about acting within your deadline to temporarily stop or delay the eviction process. Eviction removal is for a case that's already on your record — it's about pursuing expungement or sealing so it no longer shows up on background or rental checks. Many clients start with response and move to removal later.

Is this a law firm? Will someone represent me in court?

No. We're a housing and financial support service, not an attorney. We help you understand your notice, your options, and how to prepare — and we'll point you toward legal aid or an attorney referral when your situation calls for one.

What if my court date or deadline is very soon?

Call as soon as you can. Timelines vary by notice type, court, lender, and jurisdiction, and earlier contact gives you more room to act on what we cover together.

Is the listed price the total cost, or are there extra fees?

Eviction response is a flat $350, and eviction removal starts at $350 depending on your case. Credit repair runs $100 per bureau at our current sale price. Loan modification is quoted based on complexity. Any fees are agreed with you in writing before work begins — never billed as a surprise afterward.

Can I start the process online instead of calling?

Yes — eviction response and loan modification have online intake forms on this page. For eviction removal and credit repair, give us a call to set up your consultation.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

One Stop Advisory ("we," "us," or "our") provides eviction response, eviction removal, loan modification, and credit repair support services. This section explains what information we collect when you contact us or use this website, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you reach out by phone, email, text, Instagram, or our online intake form, we may collect your name, contact details, and information about your housing, court, or financial situation, including details from any notice, court record, loan document, or credit report you share with us. Payment information submitted through our intake forms is processed by our third-party form provider and is not stored on this website.

How we use it

We use the information you share only to provide the support you've requested — reviewing your situation, explaining your options, and preparing your action plan. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Confidentiality

Details about your situation are treated as confidential. We only share information with outside parties (such as a legal aid organization, court, lender, or referral attorney) with your knowledge, when doing so is part of helping you find further support.

Data retention

We keep client information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and maintain basic business records, after which it is deleted or securely discarded.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have on file for you, or request that we delete it, by emailing adminsupport@onestopadvisory.org.
