Questions people ask

Before you call, here's what to know.

Is this a law firm? Will someone represent me in court?

No. We're a housing support service, not an attorney. We help you understand your notice, your options, and how to prepare — and we'll point you toward legal aid or an attorney referral when your situation calls for one.

What if my court date is very soon?

Call as soon as you can. Timelines vary by notice type and jurisdiction, and earlier contact gives you more room to act on what we cover together.

Is the $350 the total cost, or are there extra fees?

$350 is the full flat rate for the support described above. If your situation needs something beyond that scope, we'll always tell you before any additional cost, not after.

Do I need to have all my paperwork ready to call?

No. Bring whatever you have — even a photo of the notice is fine to start. We'll tell you what else to gather once we know your situation.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

One Stop Advisory ("we," "us," or "our") provides eviction response support services. This section explains what information we collect when you contact us or use this website, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you reach out by phone, email, text, or Instagram, we may collect your name, contact details, and information about your housing situation, including details from any notice you share with us. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

We use the information you share only to provide the eviction response support you've requested — reviewing your notice, explaining your options, and preparing your action plan. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Confidentiality

Details about your housing situation are treated as confidential. We only share information with outside parties (such as a legal aid organization or referral attorney) with your knowledge, when doing so is part of helping you find further support.

Data retention

We keep client information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and maintain basic business records, after which it is deleted or securely discarded.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have on file for you, or request that we delete it, by emailing adminsupport@onestopadvisory.org.
